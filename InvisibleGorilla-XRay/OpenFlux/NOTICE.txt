OpenFlux
========

This folder ships the OpenFlux client binary (https://github.com/p1neappleXpress/OpenFlux).
OpenFlux is licensed under GPL-3.0. Source is upstream; Gorilla launches it as a sidecar.

Do not place enc.key in a public release zip. The encryption key lives in user Settings.json
and is written to OpenFlux/enc.key at runtime.
